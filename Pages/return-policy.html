Return Policy

Last updated: December 30, 2024

1. Return Window

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return your StepStyle shoes for a full refund or exchange. Items must be returned in their original condition.

2. Eligible Items

The following items are eligible for return:

  • Shoes in original, unworn condition
  • Items with original packaging and tags
  • Shoes without signs of wear, damage, or odor
  • Items purchased at full price or on sale

Non-Returnable Items

  • Customized or personalized shoes
  • Items worn outdoors or showing signs of use
  • Shoes without original packaging or tags
  • Items damaged by misuse or normal wear

3. How to Return

1

Initiate Return

Contact our customer service team at returns@stepstyle.com or call +91 9876543210

2

Get Return Label

We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label within 24 hours

3

Pack & Ship

Pack items in original packaging and attach the return label

4

Refund Processed

Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return

4. Exchange Policy

We offer free exchanges for:

  • Size exchanges: Different size of the same shoe
  • Color exchanges: Different color of the same model
  • Defective items: Manufacturing defects or damage during shipping

Exchanges are subject to availability. If the desired item is not available, we'll process a full refund.

5. Refund Process

  • Refunds will be credited to your original payment method
  • Processing time: 5-7 business days after we receive your return
  • Bank processing may take additional 3-5 business days
  • You'll receive an email confirmation when refund is processed

6. Return Shipping

  • Free returns: We provide prepaid return labels for all returns
  • International returns: Customer responsible for return shipping costs
  • Damaged/Defective items: We cover all return shipping costs

7. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive damaged or defective shoes:

  • Contact us immediately at support@stepstyle.com
  • Include photos of the damage or defect
  • We'll arrange immediate replacement or full refund
  • No need to return damaged items unless requested

8. Gift Returns

  • Gift recipients can return items without original receipt
  • Refund will be issued as store credit
  • Gift giver can provide receipt for cash refund
  • Same 30-day return window applies
