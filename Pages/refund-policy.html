Refund Policy

Last updated: December 30, 2024

1. Refund Eligibility

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • Items are returned within 30 days of delivery
  • Shoes are in original, unworn condition
  • Original packaging and tags are included
  • Items show no signs of wear, damage, or odor

2. Refund Processing Time

1-2 Days

Return Received

We inspect your returned items

3-5 Days

Refund Approved

Refund is processed to your original payment method

5-10 Days

Refund Credited

Amount appears in your account (varies by bank)

3. Refund Methods

Credit/Debit Cards

Refunded to original card within 5-7 business days

UPI Payments

Instant refund to your UPI account

Digital Wallets

Refunded to original wallet within 1-3 business days

Net Banking

Refunded to your bank account within 5-7 business days

4. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued for:

  • Items returned after 30 days but within 60 days (50% refund)
  • Items with minor signs of wear (assessed case by case)
  • Items missing original packaging (₹200 deduction)
  • Items returned without original tags (₹100 deduction)

5. No Refund Situations

Refunds will not be provided for:

  • Items returned after 60 days
  • Customized or personalized shoes
  • Items damaged by misuse or normal wear
  • Shoes worn outdoors or showing significant wear
  • Items purchased with store credit (exchange only)

6. Cancellation Refunds

Before Shipping

  • 100% refund if cancelled within 2 hours of order
  • Full refund minus processing fee (₹50) if cancelled before shipping

After Shipping

  • Standard return policy applies
  • Customer responsible for return shipping if order is refused

7. Defective Item Refunds

For manufacturing defects or shipping damage:

  • 100% refund plus return shipping costs
  • Expedited refund processing (1-3 business days)
  • Option for immediate replacement
  • No inspection period required

8. International Refunds

  • Refunds processed in original currency
  • Currency conversion rates may vary
  • International bank fees may apply
  • Processing time: 7-14 business days

9. Refund Tracking

Track your refund status:

  • Email notifications at each step
  • SMS updates for refund processing
  • Customer portal access for real-time status
  • Reference number provided for bank inquiries
